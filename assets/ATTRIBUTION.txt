DEADLIGHT character and weapon assets

zombie_original (embedded in index.html as data)
  Original character created for this project: AI-generated concept art and
  base mesh, with hand-authored rigging, texturing, and animation
  (Idle, Walk, Walk2 limp, Run, Run2 feral sprint, Attack, Attack2 swipe,
  Attack3 overhead slam, HitReact, HitReact2, Crawl, Climb window vault,
  Death). No third-party models, textures, or animation clips.

first-person arms / knife / grenade & magazine props (in index.html)
  Original procedural viewmodel rig (arms, gloved hands, combat knife,
  grenade and magazine props) built in code for this project, with
  hand-authored reload, knife-slash and grenade-throw motions.
  No third-party models, textures, or animation clips.

gun models: sidearm, viper, breacher, sentinel, warden, arc9, mauler,
longbow, nova (embedded in index.html as data)
  Original weapon models created for this project: AI-generated concept
  art and base meshes, with textures projected from the original concept
  art. Barrel convention -Z, "Muzzle" locator at each barrel tip for
  tracers/flash. No third-party models or textures.
